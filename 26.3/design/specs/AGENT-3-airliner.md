# AGENT-3: the mini airliner (Minecraft 26.3 line)

You implement the flight model of the mini airliner: an 11-block passenger jet with a pilot and five
passengers, heavy, slow to turn, needing a long take-off run, with a pitch clamp so it cannot
tail-strike on a normal rotation. The entity (with six seats, the logo roll and the metal-skin rule), the
item, the models, the renderer (skin switch and logo), the registration and the headless test command
already exist on your base branch (foundation agent); you replace the stub numbers, verify the seats,
skin and logo behaviour, and prove the numbers on your own test server. Design: `26.3/design/DESIGN.md`
(§1.4, §2, §4, §8.3, §11.1); plan: `26.3/design/PLAN.md`; read `26.3/design/reports/AGENT-1-REPORT.md`
first.

### Why these values

- **Tail strike.** The model rotates about entity `(0, 0.375, 0)`; the aft end of the keel is at entity
  `(y 0.8125, z -3.25)`, the three tail-cone steps at `(1.0625, -4.0)`, `(1.3125, -4.625)`, `(1.625, -5.25)`.
  Nose-up by θ lowers a point `(dy, dz)` relative to the pivot to `0.375 + dy cos θ + dz sin θ`; it
  touches the ground at `sin θ = (0.375 + dy) / -dz`: keel 14.5 deg, tail_1 15.4, tail_2 16.5, tail_3 18.
  The clamp at 12 deg leaves 2.5 deg; `PlaneEntity.tickPitch` applies it while `getOnGround()` and
  releases it when the coyote timer expires after lift-off.
- **Lift-off at 12 deg.** The velocity vector's climb per tick is `pitchToMotion * d * (xRot - pitch) +
  maxLift * liftRatio` against gravity's `atan(0.03 / v)`; at 0.70 b/t that is `0.16*12 + 2.0*0.83 = 3.6`
  deg/t against 2.45, so the aircraft leaves the ground at about 0.70 b/t with the nose on the clamp. With
  the stock 48x ground drag the airliner capped at 0.64 b/t on the ground and never lifted off; hence
  `groundLinearDragFactor 5` and the constant rolling resistance, which together produce the long run.
- **Turn.** 0.875 deg/t nominal, 0.49 realised (10 deg/s), radius 117 b at 1.0 b/t: between the large
  plane (1.25 nominal) and the cargo plane (0.5).

### Seats, skin, logo (verify, do not redesign)

- Six seats on the centre line at `z = 4.25, 2.34375, 1.21875, 0.09375, -1.03125, -2.15625`, `y = 0.375`.
- Skin: `hasMetalSkin()` is true when the material block is in the tag `simpleplanes:airliner_metal_skin`
  (iron, copper, waxed copper, gold, netherite). The workbench accepts them because the foundation added
  them to `plane_materials`.
- Logo: synched `LOGO` 0..5, rolled once, saved as `Logo`, kept through the item.

## Integration

- `entities/AirlinerEntity.java`: the numbers above, `readAdditionalSaveData`/`addAdditionalSaveData`
  as the foundation left them.
- `commands/AirlinerCommand.java` (`/airliner`, permission 2): `status` prints, for every airliner in
  loaded chunks, `#id logo=<n> skin=<metal|wood> riders=<n> pos= spd= pitch= og=`; `board <id>` mounts up
  to five nearby villagers (within 8 b; summon them first) as passengers 1..5 for the rider tests and
  reports the seat index of each; `logo <id> <0..5>` sets the logo (test aid).
- The autopilot recognises `type airliner` (foundation). Its runway-length requirement
  (`AUTOPILOT.md` "How much runway an aircraft actually needs") is computed from the airframe's rates; if a
  183-block runway is refused for the airliner, report it with the number the survey prints rather than
  tuning the autopilot.
